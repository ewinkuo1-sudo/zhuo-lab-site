        All systems are built or configured in-house and are available to collaborators after training.

        ![Schematic beam path of the multiphoton scanning microscope](gen/facilities-beampath.svg "Simplified beam path of the multiphoton platform: femtosecond excitation with polarisation control, scanner, objective, and forward plus epi detection channels. Schematic, not to scale.")

        ### Multiphoton scanning microscope (SHG / THG / two-photon fluorescence)
        - Femtosecond excitation at 1030 nm; second-harmonic, third-harmonic and two-photon fluorescence collected **simultaneously** on separate channels.
        - Resonant-plus-galvo scanning, video-rate imaging above 20 frames per second.
        - Field of view about 300 µm with a 20× objective; automated Z-stacks and mosaic stitching for whole-section coverage.
        - Forward and backward (epi) detection paths, enabling polarization-resolved SHG measurements.

        ### Confocal laser-scanning microscope
        - Olympus FluoView FV300 on an inverted microscope body, reflectance and fluorescence modes, 20×/0.40 objective; used for correlative imaging alongside the multiphoton system.

        ### Fluorescence-lifetime (FLIM) and Raman detection chain
        - Frequency-domain detection: photomultiplier, low-noise current preamplifier and RF I/Q homodyne demodulation, built for lifetime and coherent-Raman contrast.
        - Fibre femtosecond laser, 250 fs pulses at 780 nm, 40 MHz repetition rate.
        - Scientific CMOS camera (82 % peak quantum efficiency, 100 frames per second) for wide-field and camera-based detection.

        ### Sample preparation and analysis
        - Cryostat sectioning; unstained and H&E adjacent sections prepared for label-free imaging with pathologist ground truth.
        - GPU workstation for image registration, fibre-orientation quantification, lifetime fitting and deep-learning training.

        Collaborators and students from other groups who wish to use the instruments should
        [contact Dr. Zhuo](../contact/) to arrange access and training.
