The complete list is on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=nF1TaA8AAAAJ) and [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-9813-2989). To see quickly what the lab does, start with these five:

1. **Telling esophageal squamous cell carcinoma from high-grade dysplasia.** SHG and two-photon fluorescence image the extracellular matrix; texture features and a support-vector machine reach 75% to 95.65% accuracy across four comparisons. *Scientific Reports* 2025 · [10.1038/s41598-025-13910-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-13910-7)
2. **Deep learning separates normal and tumor prostate tissue.** On 295 label-free two-photon fluorescence images, deep learning reaches 86.1% accuracy against 71.9% for texture features (cross-validation). *Scientific Reports* 2026 · [10.1038/s41598-026-79760-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-79760-z)
3. **The worst pattern of invasion in oral cancer.** Clinical data from 263 patients plus multimodal nonlinear microscopy: in the more invasive samples, collagen fibers tend to align perpendicular to the tumor boundary. *Laboratory Investigation* 2026 · [10.1016/j.labinv.2025.104257](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.labinv.2025.104257)
4. **Polarization-resolved SHG compares healthy and diseased cartilage.** Normal, osteoarthritic and rheumatoid-arthritis cartilage differ in susceptibility ratio, degree of linear polarization and fiber orientation. *APL Bioengineering* 2024 · [10.1063/5.0196676](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0196676)
5. **Seeing the chirality of collagen with SHG circular dichroism.** The methodological starting point for much of the lab's collagen work. *Biomedical Optics Express* 2013 · [10.1364/boe.4.000909](https://doi.org/10.1364/boe.4.000909)
