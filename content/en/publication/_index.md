2. **Deep learning on label-free two-photon images.** Deep and classical machine learning are combined to classify tissue directly from multiphoton images, without staining. *IEEE Photonics Journal* 2026 · [10.1109/jphot.2026.3701723](https://doi.org/10.1109/jphot.2026.3701723)
3. **Reading the worst pattern of invasion in oral cancer.** Label-free multiphoton imaging applied to a prognostic pathology feature that is hard to score by eye. *Laboratory Investigation* 2026 · [10.1016/j.labinv.2025.104257](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.labinv.2025.104257)
4. **Polarization-resolved SHG tells healthy from diseased cartilage.** Collagen fibre orientation and susceptibility ratios distinguish normal, osteoarthritic and rheumatoid cartilage. *APL Bioengineering* 2024 · [10.1063/5.0196676](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0196676)
