Macline Crecsilla Lewis is a biomedical engineer and AI/ML specialist pursuing a Ph.D. at the Institute of Biophotonics, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University. Her research focuses on high-resolution imaging of glioblastoma tissue and on accelerating multiphoton imaging with artificial intelligence.

She develops detection, classification and segmentation pipelines to support intraoperative imaging and clinical decision-making. By combining biomedical imaging with deep learning, she aims to improve the speed and precision of cancer imaging and surgical guidance.
