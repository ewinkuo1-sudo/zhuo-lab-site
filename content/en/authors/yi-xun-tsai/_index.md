Yi-Shun Tsai is a master's student at the Institute of Biophotonics, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University. His research combines machine learning and deep learning with advanced cellular image analysis.

With a professional background as a licensed radiologic technologist, he brings clinical medical-imaging experience to computational computer vision for biological and diagnostic applications. His lab work also includes polarization-resolved SHG and chi-tensor analysis.
