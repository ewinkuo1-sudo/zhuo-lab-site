Guan-Yu Zhuo is an Associate Professor at the Institute of Biophotonics, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University. He received his Ph.D. in Physics from National Taiwan University in 2012 for work on nonlinear optical microscopy, and held postdoctoral positions at the Department of Bioengineering, Caltech, the Institute of Atomic and Molecular Sciences, Academia Sinica, and the Max Planck Institute for Polymer Research. Before joining NYCU he was on the faculty of National Sun Yat-sen University and China Medical University.

His research advances label-free optical imaging, in particular multimodal nonlinear optical microscopy, polarization-resolved second-harmonic generation and nonlinear endomicroscopy, with the aim of bringing these tools into clinical diagnosis. He was named an Excellent Young Scholar of Taiwan's 2030 Cross-Generation Young Scholars Program in 2024 and is a co-editor of *Advanced Biophysical Techniques in Biosciences* (Springer, 2025).
