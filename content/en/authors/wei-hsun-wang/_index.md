Wei-Hsun Wang is a master's student whose research advances nonlinear optical microscopy through circuit design and signal enhancement. He studies time stretching of femtosecond second-harmonic-generation (SHG) signals using quantum-dot-mediated absorption and re-emission to improve signal intensity and the signal-to-background ratio.

The supplied lab profile reports a 77% increase in SHG signal intensity in three-dimensional collagen-fibre samples, alongside improved image quality and reduced photodamage. He is extending this approach toward faster lifetime imaging and Raman imaging for biomedical research and clinical applications.
