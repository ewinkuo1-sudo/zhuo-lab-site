Jun studies the biochemical and structural characterization of cartilage using nonlinear optical imaging. The work combines polarization-resolved second-harmonic generation (PSHG) and Raman spectroscopy to distinguish normal cartilage, osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis.

This research develops label-free, high-resolution tools for understanding cartilage pathology and supporting diagnostic and therapeutic research in musculoskeletal disorders.
